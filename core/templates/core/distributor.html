Eligibility

  • Valid GST registration.
  • Wholesale drug licence: Form 20B and Form 21B, as applicable to the products and your business.
  • Adequate storage facilities.
  • Existing pharmaceutical distribution experience.
  • An established network of retail chemists.
  • Financial capacity to manage distribution.

Business & sales support

  • Sales representatives.
  • Help connecting with local chemists and pharmacies.
  • Promotional materials, product catalogues and marketing support.
  • Retailer visits and help generating orders from retail pharmacies.
  • Sales and business guidance.
