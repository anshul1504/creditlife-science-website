Quality & compliance

How we keep every pack clear, traceable and safe to use.

Licensed manufacturing

Our products are manufactured in India under valid manufacturing licences. The licence number is printed on the pack.

Traceable batches

Every pack carries its batch number, manufacturing date, expiry date and MRP so each unit can be traced.

Clear, bilingual labelling

Composition, directions and cautions appear in English and Hindi to support correct use.

Responsible communication

Prescription medicines are presented to healthcare professionals only, without promotional claims to the public.

Storage guidance

Each product page lists storage conditions so quality is maintained from the stockist to the shelf.

Report a side effect

If you experience an unexpected effect, contact your doctor and write to us so we can follow up.
